Tap a name to highlight only that country; tap an area of the map for its source data. ◆ marks a strategic point such as a strait, canal or base, whose ruler at this time was checked separately. A dotted ring (◌) means the map colour differs from who actually ruled it.
Border data: historical-basemaps (André Ourednik et al., GPL-3.0) — simplified, with clear errors in sovereignty labels corrected year by year (see ‘Data corrections’ for each year). The source is a work in progress, and borders before the modern era are approximations of spheres of influence. ‘Border precision’ in the area details is the source's own value (1 rough · 2 medium · 3 precise). The rulers of the 19 ◆ strategic points (straits, canals, bases) were checked year by year independently of the map data; where they disagree with the map colour, this is listed under ‘Strategic points’ in the panel. Because the gaps between years are uneven, ‘Gains and losses’ covers the main events since the previous year shown.
